# Results

**Summary:** After extensive testing and data collection, the precise root cause of the previous network issues remains uncertain. However, it appears that **IT interventions have significantly reduced** the inconsistencies experienced last semester. Connectivity and speed tests show improved stability and more predictable performance overall.

**Current State:**  
While the network now performs more reliably under typical conditions, it still exhibits **vulnerability during peak usage periods**. When a large number of students connect simultaneously, particularly in areas served by too few access points, network speeds and responsiveness still suffer.

**Key Observations:**
- **Improved Baseline:** General connectivity and throughput metrics are noticeably better than last semester’s figures.  
- **Peak Load Issues:** High-density usage scenarios still reveal bottlenecks, suggesting that the primary issue may be insufficient access point distribution or limited bandwidth allocation.  
- **Future Considerations:** Additional hardware or optimized configurations may be needed to ensure consistently strong performance, even during peak usage.

In conclusion, while progress has been made and the network is generally more stable, there remains room for improvement to handle the demands of a growing user base.
